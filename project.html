Focus Crops in Our Rotation Plan

For our Community Service Project, we focused on four specific plant families to demonstrate a balanced rotation cycle. By alternating these crops, farmers can naturally replenish soil nitrogen, break up hard soil, and prevent pest build-up without relying on chemical fertilizers.

1. Corn (Heavy Feeder)

Corn requires high amounts of nitrogen to grow tall and strong, making it the perfect crop to plant *after* legumes have enriched the soil.

2. Soybeans (Legumes)

Legumes act as natural fertilizers. They pull nitrogen directly from the air and fix it into the soil roots, preparing the land for the next season.

3. Carrots (Root Crops)

Root vegetables dig deep into the earth, naturally aerating and breaking up compacted soil so water can reach deeper.

4. Spinach (Leafy Greens)

Leafy greens have shallow roots and grow quickly, acting as a great "rest" crop for the soil before restarting the heavy feeding cycle.
